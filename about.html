About Pandit Computers & Printers

Fifteen years of honest service, one customer at a time.

Our Story

From a Small Photocopy Counter to a Full-Service Hub

Pandit Computers & Printers opened its doors in 2011 as a modest two-machine photocopy stall in 684/18 Kailashpuri, Bulandshahr — run by Mr. R.K. Pandit with a simple belief: every customer deserves their job done right, the first time.

Over the years, as Bulandshahr grew, so did we. What started with photocopies now covers 44 services — from high-speed Konica digital printing and Komori offset press work to Aadhaar updates, PAN cards, laptop repairs, and eco-solvent banner printing. Same address, same honesty, much bigger capability.

Today we are proud to be Bulandshahr's most trusted one-stop shop — equipped with industry-grade machines, a skilled team, and the personal attention that built our reputation since day one.
