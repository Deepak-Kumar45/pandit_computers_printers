Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll answer personally.

What are your shop timings?▾
We're open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and Sunday 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Walk-ins are always welcome.
Do you accept bulk printing orders?▾
Yes, we regularly handle bulk orders for businesses, schools, and events — including visiting cards, brochures, and ID cards — with discounted bulk pricing.
Can I send my files online before visiting?▾
Absolutely. Use our Print Order Form on the Contact page to upload your files in advance — your order will be ready when you arrive.
Do you help with Aadhaar and PAN card corrections?▾
Yes, we offer full assistance with Aadhaar updates, enrollment, and PAN card applications/corrections, guiding you through the official process.
What payment methods do you accept?▾
We accept cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, and net banking for your convenience.
How fast can I get a banner printed?▾
Standard banners are typically ready within 2-4 hours. Urgent same-hour service is available for an additional rush fee, subject to workload.
Do you offer home delivery?▾
Yes, we offer local delivery for bulk orders and select services. Delivery charges depend on distance and order size — ask our staff for details.
Can you repair both desktops and laptops?▾
Yes, our technicians service both desktops and laptops of all major brands, covering hardware faults, software issues, and virus removal.
What file formats do you accept for printing?▾
We accept PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX, and PPTX files. For best results, PDF format is recommended to preserve formatting.
Do you provide design services for visiting cards and brochures?▾
Yes, our design team can create custom layouts for visiting cards, brochures, banners, and more, or refine designs you already have.
How long does ID card printing take?▾
Standard ID card orders are usually completed within a few hours. Large batches (50+ cards) may take until the next business day.
Can I cancel or modify an order after placing it?▾
Orders can be modified or cancelled before printing begins. Once printing has started, changes may not be possible — contact us immediately if needed.
Do you offer student discounts?▾
Yes, we offer special discounted rates on photocopying and printing for students with valid ID.
Is lamination available for all document sizes?▾
We laminate A4, A5, ID card size, and custom sizes. Let us know your document dimensions and we'll match the right film.
Can you help me book railway or flight tickets urgently?▾
Yes, we offer ticket booking assistance for trains, buses, and flights, though availability depends on the booking platform at the time.
Do I need to bring my own documents for Aadhaar/PAN services?▾
Yes, please bring original supporting documents (proof of identity/address) as required by UGAI/NSDL guidelines for the specific service.
What's the difference between flex and vinyl printing?▾
Flex is a cost-effective option ideal for banners and signage, while vinyl offers a more durable, premium finish suited for vehicle graphics and long-term outdoor use.
Can you recover data from a crashed computer?▾
In many cases, yes. Our technicians can attempt data recovery from damaged or crashed drives — success depends on the extent of the damage.
Do you install genuine licensed software?▾
Yes, we only install and assist with properly licensed software including Windows and MS Office, ensuring you stay compliant and secure.
How do I track my print order?▾
Once your order is placed, you'll receive an order ID. Use our Track Order page to check its current status anytime.
Is there a minimum order quantity for visiting cards?▾
Our standard visiting card packages start at a box of 100 cards, though smaller custom quantities can be discussed with our team.
Do you offer same-day passport photo printing?▾
Yes, passport and ID-size photos are clicked and printed instantly on the spot — no appointment needed.
