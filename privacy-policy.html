Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Pandit Computers & Printers ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, place an order, or book a service, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, and details relevant to your request, including uploaded files for print orders.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to process orders, respond to inquiries, confirm bookings, and occasionally send updates if you've subscribed to our newsletter. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Document & File Handling

Files uploaded for printing are used only to complete your order and are deleted from our systems after the job is finished, unless you request otherwise.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to remember preferences such as dark mode settings. No personally identifiable tracking cookies are used.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us directly.
