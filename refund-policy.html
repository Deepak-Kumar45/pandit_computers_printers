Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

We want every customer to be satisfied with their order. This policy outlines when refunds or reprints are available.

Printing Errors on Our Part

If a printed item contains an error caused by our team (incorrect color, misprint, or production defect not matching the approved proof), we will reprint the item free of charge or issue a refund.

Customer-Approved Files

Once a design or document has been approved by the customer and printed exactly as approved, refunds are not available for content or design changes requested afterward.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund before production begins. Once printing or processing has started, cancellation may not be possible, or may incur a partial charge for materials and labor already used.

Digital & Government Services

Service fees for assistance with forms, Aadhaar/PAN applications, and similar services cover our staff's time and effort, and are non-refundable once the service has been performed, regardless of the outcome from the government authority.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us within 48 hours of order completion with your order ID and details of the issue. We will review and respond within 2 business days.
